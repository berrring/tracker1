# Product requirements

SafeTrack supports local demonstration and a separate authenticated family workspace. A verified email account can create a family, invite another verified account, review incoming invitations and accept or decline. Family owners can revoke member access. Invitations are available in the recipient’s signed-in inbox.

The demo retains the map, events, device simulator and local USB setup. The accounts workspace contains no simulated connected trackers or child locations. Device status and pairing tables reserve room for later authorized functionality.
